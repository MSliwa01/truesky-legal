Privacy Policy

TrueSky · Last updated: 19 October 2026

TrueSky shows an illustrated weather forecast for a place you choose. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking across apps or websites.

Who is responsible

The controller of personal data in TrueSky is Mateusz Śliwa, Poland, the developer of the app. Contact: sliwa.mateo@gmail.com.

What leaves your device, and why

DataSent toPurpose
The text you type in city searchOpen-Meteo Geocoding API; Apple Maps search only while Open-Meteo is unavailableFinding matching places
Coordinates of the selected placeOpen-Meteo Forecast API; Apple Weather (WeatherKit) only while Open-Meteo is unavailableDownloading the forecast
Your current position, only if you allow location accessApple (system geocoder)Finding the name of the place you are in
An anonymous app user ID and purchase receiptsRevenueCat, AppleUnlocking and restoring TrueSky Pro

Like any internet request, these services also receive your IP address. See the Open-Meteo terms, the Apple Weather data sources, the RevenueCat privacy policy and the Apple privacy policy.

TrueSky reads your position only while you pick a place: on the place step of the first setup, or when you tap “Use my location”. It never reads it in the background. That position then becomes your selected place: its coordinates are saved on your device like any place you pick, and used for its forecast and weather alerts. They are not sent to any server of mine, because I don’t run one.

Stored on your device only

The selected place (its name and coordinates, which are your position if you chose “Use my location”), the last downloaded forecast (for offline use) and your settings (language, units, landscape, sound, notification choices). Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Weather alerts (rain, storm, frost, heat) are created on your device from the forecast for your selected place and shown as local notifications. There is no push server. You can turn alerts off in the app or in the system settings.

Payments

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. TrueSky never receives your payment details. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your App Store account settings at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Legal basis and your rights

The data above is used only to provide the features you ask for: search, the forecast and TrueSky Pro (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Your current position is used only if you allow location access (Art. 6(1)(a)); you can withdraw that in the iPhone Settings at any time.

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. I don't hold data that identifies you by name. To ask about your purchase records at RevenueCat, or to have them deleted, write to the address above. You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (uodo.gov.pl).

Children

TrueSky is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Contact: sliwa.mateo@gmail.com.
