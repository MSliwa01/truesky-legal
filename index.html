Frequently asked

Where does the forecast come from?
From Open-Meteo. The landscape illustrates the hourly forecast for the place you choose; it is a painting, not a photo of that place.

How do I restore TrueSky Pro?
Settings → TrueSky Pro → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription?
iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → TrueSky. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Weather alerts do not arrive.
Allow notifications for TrueSky in the iPhone Settings and keep the alerts switched on in the app settings.
